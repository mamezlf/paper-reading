Same Surface Form ≠ Same Meaning
Token	Japanese	Chinese
学生	student	student
経済	economy	economy
手紙	letter	toilet paper
愛人	lover	spouse / kept partner
Research Question

When Japanese and Chinese share the same kanji token,

Should semantically similar kanji be shared?
Should semantically different kanji be shared?
Hypothesis

If overlap mainly works through semantic transfer,

then:

High-similarity kanji sharing
→ should improve alignment

Low-similarity kanji sharing
→ may weaken alignment or introduce interference
